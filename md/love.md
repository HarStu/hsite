# Favorites
_& short thoughts on each_

## Novels
_Broadly, I enjoy novels focused on subtle, naturalistic interiority. I think literature's main value is in guiding the reader to a unique, unnameable emotion they will carry forever._

Fifteen Dogs
- rending, unblinking, and full of love. read in a single sitting, punctuated by bouts of ugly crying. i have one of prince's poems committed to memory. 

The Once and Future King
- few other characters have moved me as much as white's lancelot. "he felt in his heart cruelty and cowardice, the things which made him brave and kind"

Stoner
- as close to a perfect novel as anyone has ever come. left me speechlessly grateful for having had the chance to study literature. 

The Road
- an old favorite that i will not return to until i'm a father. 

Never Let Me Go
- ishiguro is my favorite author, and i love everything he's written. never let me go may not be the most subtle or artistically ambitious novel he's written, but it's the one I cried the hardest over. it stands for him on this list. 

Norweigan Wood
- nothing else is so true about being twenty. 


## Nonfiction, Spirituality, Theory, Essays
_Models I return to_

Meditation from Cold Start to Complete Mastery: a Manual of Global Wayfinding Meditation
- closer to a totalizing memetic object than anything else i've ever explored. gets it. 

A Path With Heart
- as close to a wise friend as a book can be

The Age of Surveillance Capitalism
- defines a remarkably useful vocabulary for talking about the relationship between tech companies and the public.

...Three, Two, One, Contact: Times Square Red
- delaney's distinction between 'contact' and 'networking' interactions is the sort of fork you'll see everywhere forever

Three Hundred Ways It Can Hurt To Be a Man
- a rare, comprehensive, empathetic reflection on the way men struggle. too much writing on men's issues is reactionary or neurotically hedged. while i don't agree with all three hundred, i'm deeply grateful this document exists. it made me feel loved and seen in a way little else has.

## Games
_Games are broadly wasted on gamers_

Street Fighter III: Third Strike
- i played competitively for a number of years in my early 20's. urien main. retired after placing 17th out of ~1104 at EVO 2024. 

Rain World
- a singularly powerful artistic accomplishment. raw and gorgeous. ignore the dlc.

Spelunky
- a perfectly-tuned risk-vs-reward driven sandbox. 

Nuclear Throne
- game-as-visceral-sensory-pleasure overdose

Dark Souls
- everything that can be said about it has been. it really is that good. 

## Misc
_Other things, sillier things_

The Toronto Blue Jays
- how can you not be romantic about baseball?

Hunter x Hunter
- shonen manga was formative for me, and HxH is the finest example of the genre. togashi's panel composition is second to none.

r!animorphs
- remarkable xenofiction in desperate need of an editor. read in a delirious frenzy after a breakup. i don't put fanfiction on this list lightly; it fought its way here against all odds.
